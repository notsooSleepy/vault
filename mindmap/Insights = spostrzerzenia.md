# Insights
* deep understanding of a business issue or situation to uncover value
1. Collect information
2. Organize information
3. Analyze information
4. Determine action plan
5. Communicate action plan
6. Observe outcome
## Best practices
* always keep key business objectives in mind
* tie insights back to common business objectives for better context
* simplicity is essential. Break complex insights into smaller ideas
# Audience
* understand the audience's expected format
* tailor the message based on audience feedback
* keep the messaging simple
* utilize context to enhance value
## Key questions
* what roles does your audience have?
* what does your audience already know?
* what does your audience need to know?
* how well does your audience know you?
