# Transformer
## Architecture
* emphasizes long-range relationships between words in a sentence to generate accurate and coherent text
## Components
### Pre-processing
* breaks down sentences into individual tokens
* presents them in numerical form
### Positional Encoding
* provides information about the position of words in a sequence
* helps transformer tie together distant words
### Encoders
* includes attention mechanism that directs attention to specific words and their relationships
* neural networks
### Decoders
* also uses attention and neural networks
