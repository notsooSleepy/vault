# Diagnostic analytics
* *why* is it happening
* find root cause of events
## why to use?
* find potential causes of events or reasons for behaviors
* investigate causal relationships
* suggest solutions based on the identified causes
### caution
* *diagnostic analytics can suggest causes, but not prove causality on its own*
